rid">
    <div class="effect"><strong>More stable TN</strong>Supports a wider safety margin for Total Nitrogen.</div>

    <div class="effect"><strong>Lower additional cost</strong>Reduces the risk of unclogging, repair, and replacement of lost carrier media.</div>
    <div class="effect"><strong>Support discharge compliance</strong>Improves system stability and TN safety margin.</div>
