<section id="en-solution">
  <h2 class="section-title">4. SOLUTION — COMPARISON OF 3 BIO-CARRIER TYPES</h2>
  <p class="section-note">Each option is explained as: <b>Simple explanation → Advantages → Disadvantages</b>.</p>
  <div class="solution-grid">
    <div class="media-card">
      <div class="media-photo"><img src="" data-img="3" alt="sphere"></div>
      <div class="media-body">
        <h3>1. Spherical carrier</h3>
        <div class="media-desc"><b>Simple explanation:</b> a loose carrier, larger than the current type.</div>
        <div class="box-label good">Advantages</div>
        <ul><li>Easy to add and replace.</li><li>Larger size → less likely to pass through small gaps.</li><li>Can support nitrogen-removal bacteria.</li></ul>
        <div class="box-label bad">Disadvantages</div>
        <ul><li>Still a loose carrier → drifting risk remains.</li><li>A retaining screen is required.</li><li>If it escapes, it can still cause clogging.</li></ul>
        <div class="result-tag">Assessment: INTERMEDIATE OPTION</div>
      </div>
    </div>

    <div class="media-card recommend">
      <div class="media-photo contain"><img src="" data-img="4" alt="fiber"></div>
      <div class="media-body">
        <h3>2. Fiber carrier</h3>
        <div class="media-desc"><b>Simple explanation:</b> fibers are fixed to a frame and do not move freely in the tank.</div>
        <div class="box-label good">Advantages</div>
        <ul><li>Almost no drifting when properly fixed.</li><li>Very low risk of clogging pipes, pumps, or MBR.</li><li>Stable biomass attachment; suitable to support TN reduction.</li><li>Suitable for application in Aerotank and Anoxic tanks.</li></ul>
        <div class="box-label bad">Disadvantages</div>
        <ul><li>A fixing frame is required.</li><li>Fiber spacing must be properly arranged.</li><li>Initial installation takes longer than loose media.</li></ul>
        <div class="result-tag best">Assessment: RECOMMENDED OPTION</div>
      </div>
    </div>

    <div class="media-card">
      <div class="media-photo"><img src="" data-img="5" alt="small"></div>
      <div class="media-body">
        <h3>3. Small attached-growth carrier</h3>
        <div class="media-desc"><b>Simple explanation:</b> this is the type currently in use.</div>
        <div class="box-label good">Advantages</div>
        <ul><li>Microorganisms attach easily.</li><li>Easy to add to the tank.</li><li>Currently supports ammonium treatment well.</li></ul>
        <div class="box-label bad">Disadvantages</div>
        <ul><li>Breakage has already occurred.</li><li>Small pieces can drift into pipes.</li><li>Risk of pump clogging and impact on MBR.</li></ul>
        <div class="result-tag no">Assessment: NOT RECOMMENDED TO CONTINUE</div>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>
